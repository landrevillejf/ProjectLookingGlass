Mouse Gestures and Navigation

The 3D desktop is driven by a small set of consistent mouse idioms. Once you learn them, every window behaves the same way.

On a window's title bar

GestureAction
Left-dragMove the window.
Middle-dragRotate (spin) the window.
CTRL + left-dragRotate the window (alternative).
Right-clickFlip the window to its sticky-note back.

On the desktop background

GestureAction
Right-click Park windows onto the bookshelf / bring them back.
Click a parked windowRestore that window.

Inside a window's content

Clicks and drags inside the content area go to the application itself (select text, press buttons, scroll). Gestures are intentionally not taken from the content, so the application keeps full control of its own input. To move or turn a window, always use its title bar or border.

Tip: A window's pickable border is a second handle: you can flip a window from its edge as well as from the title bar.
